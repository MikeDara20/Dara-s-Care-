### About This App
Dara's Care is the app I’m building for parents and children. 
The idea: A simple, safe place where fathers can track care, notes, and memories for their kids. 
Version 1 will start small - just notes + reminders. But Kingdom Land starts small.

### Why "Dara's Care"?
Named after my daughter Dara. She taught me what care really means. 
Every feature I build here, I ask: "Will this make Dara + Fumi proud?"
This repo holds my promise to them: I will build us a home.

### My Story
I started coding with nothing but my phone. 
4 years of struggle. Sleeping in uncompleted buildings at 18. 
Industrial cleaning jobs for ₦900/day. 
Today I’m building apps from Egypt. 
Every line of code here is proof: broken floors don’t mean broken dreams.
